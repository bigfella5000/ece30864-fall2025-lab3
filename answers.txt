a) Two pros of Agile development are that it is very flexible and it delivers continuous improvements.
   Two cons are that it generally does not emphasize good documentation and it can be harder to scale.

b) The Scrum Master is in charge of facilitating work and coaching the developers below them.
   The Product Owner is in charge decision-making, strategizing, and managing what is put on the backlog.

c) Sprint planning meeting: Determine what needs to be completed in the coming spring; Determine story points and assign work
   Daily scrum meeting: Discuss work completed so far; Discuss what needs to be completed for the day and any issues
   Sprint retrospective meeting: Discuss the completed work; Move incompleted work to backlog

d) Epic: A large body of work that can be split up into stories
   Story: A software requirement that can be expressed in a few sentences
   Bug: An issue in your software that needs to be fixed
   Task: A unit of work smaller than a story that is needed in order to complete its parent story. It often refers to a small bit of technical work.
